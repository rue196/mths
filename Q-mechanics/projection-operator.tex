\section*{Proof of Invariance for the Spinor Projection Operator $\Pi$}

\textbf{Theorem.} 
The projection operator $\Pi$, defined via a rank-6 Levi-Civita contraction on a 12-vertex configuration in $\mathbb{R}^6$, is well-defined, orientation-consistent, and invariant under global spinor rotation $e^{i\theta}$.

\begin{proof}
Let the 12 vertices be embedded in $\mathbb{R}^6$.  
Complexify each coordinate pair to define spinors


\[
z_{ij} = \hat{x}_{ij} + i\hat{y}_{ij} \in \mathbb{C}.
\]


The global spinor rotation acts by
\begin{equation}
    \Psi_\theta : z_{ij} \mapsto e^{i\theta} z_{ij},
\end{equation}
which induces a linear orthogonal transformation on each $\mathbb{R}^2$ coordinate plane.

The projection operator $\Pi$ is constructed using the rank-6 Levi-Civita tensor $\varepsilon_{ijklmn}$.  
Under any transformation $O \in SO(6)$, the tensor transforms as
\begin{equation}
    \varepsilon'_{abcdef}
    = \varepsilon_{ijklmn}\,
      O_{ia} O_{jb} O_{kc} O_{ld} O_{me} O_{nf}
    = \det(O)\, \varepsilon_{abcdef}.
\end{equation}
Since the spinor rotation $e^{i\theta}$ corresponds to a block-diagonal product of $SO(2)$ rotations, the induced transformation $O_{\text{total}} \in SO(6)$ satisfies


\[
\det(O_{\text{total}}) = 1.
\]


Thus the Levi-Civita contraction is invariant, and therefore


\[
\Pi \circ \Psi_\theta = \Pi.
\]



The reduction from $\mathbb{R}^6$ to $\mathbb{R}^3$ follows the structure of the covering map


\[
SU(2) \longrightarrow SO(3).
\]


The spinor phase $\theta$ acts as the fiber of a Hopf-type fibration, restricting the effective degrees of freedom to a 3-dimensional subspace.  
Because the rotation is continuous and homotopic to the identity, orientation is preserved.

Hence, $\Pi$ is well-defined, orientation-consistent, and independent of the choice of 6D embedding.
\end{proof}
